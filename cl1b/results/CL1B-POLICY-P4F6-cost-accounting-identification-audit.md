# CL-1B P4F6 cost-accounting audit: why saved probes are not free fitness

**Secondary analysis only; no new native experiment or new holdout.** Based on the exact-source [P4F6 audit](CL1B-POLICY-P4F6-source-pinned-native-paid-clean-success-refill-542-world-audit.md), original native source `26d94bb8720f57d0593f2b0618e07e1db977759c`, [successful run 44862741694](https://github.com/iFrankStudio/cioc/actions/runs/44862741694), and [source-pinned evidence](https://github.com/iFrankStudio/cioc/tree/cl1b-ci-evidence/run-records/26d94bb8720f57d0593f2b0618e07e1db977759c-44862741694-1). This note performs transparent arithmetic on the already published aggregates; it does **not** independently reproduce the 542-world trace.

## Accounting identities and comparisons

All 180 truthful 24-stage cases completed in all three native arms, so the fixed denominator is 4,320 stages per arm. Original native CIL instructions paid: F6-on 266,149, same-source F6 PatchNext-suppressed 234,630, distinct-source F5-on 249,884. Consequently F6-on costs **31,519 additional paid instructions** relative to same-source suppressed (7.296 extra instructions/completed stage) and **16,265 additional paid instructions** relative to F5 (3.765 extra instructions/completed stage). These are sums over the selected world matrix, **not** estimates of an environment distribution or a long-lived individual.

In the **36 newly frozen** 24-stage cases, F6 costs 3,810 additional instructions relative to F5: **4.410 extra instructions per completed stage**. Its 45 fewer service probes correspond to **1.25 fewer probes per sequence** or 0.0521 per stage. Over all 180 cases, F6 saves 159 service probes versus F5 (0.883 per sequence; 0.0368 per stage), yet spends 16,265 additional instructions.

**Do not add an invented cost per probe to these instruction totals.** A real service probe is itself a charged CIL Write and its surrounding search and feedback work is already reflected in the paid-instruction counts. The descriptive ratio 16,265/159 = **102.30 additional charged instructions per fewer probe** (new cases 3,810/45 = **84.67**) is *not* a break-even energy price for a probe. Repricing a probe as an extra 103 credits would double count ordinary instruction charges unless a new, explicitly declared, external per-probe penalty is introduced. Such an external penalty would change the objective, and is not part of this experiment.

## Causal identification

The **same-source** suppression arm isolates the aggregate causal effect of permitting *all* actor-paid PatchNext changes in the F6 program, conditional on the exact tested schedules and suppression semantics. It does **not** identify the value of the quota refill alone, of PC155 monitor arming alone, or of the four-clean-stage threshold alone. The **cross-source** F5-vs-F6 comparison is a net program-performance contrast, not a randomized isolated mechanism ablation. In both comparisons, identical completion counts mean that *under the recorded charged-instruction objective* F6 is dominated in aggregate on the selected cases; this does not establish per-world dominance or prove that monitoring cannot pay off in another regime. The new wide-pair family saves 900 paid instructions versus F5, while the other three new families together lose 4,710.

The 144 previously explored sequences are not independent prospective holdouts. The 36 new sequences were frozen before native execution but their regime families are still researcher-selected. The two adversarial feedback worlds are not part of the 180 matched truthful triplets and must not be inserted into the stage-normalized comparisons.

## Falsifiable decision rule for future work (not a frozen experiment)

Before adding another monitor, define **one** primary objective: e.g. actual charged CIL per completed *true* stage, with noncompletion assigned a preregistered penalty; report true task probes separately. Freeze a prospective schedule generator and a new seed/holdout split *before* native execution. Include (i) a source-matched observer-on/off comparison, (ii) a cheap fixed monitor with matched source occupancy and private credits, and (iii) an always-off baseline. For threshold claims, compare at least two thresholds using separately frozen executable CIL sources and label them as different-program comparisons unless the same source changes its threshold by charged, observed, actor-owned code edits. Report time spent observing, policy-change costs, edit counts, true stage completion, and noncompletion; independently replay every original CIL opcode. If F6's cost overhead persists, **reject** a general net-fitness claim even if its self-edit budget successfully refills.

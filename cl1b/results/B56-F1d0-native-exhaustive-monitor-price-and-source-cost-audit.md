## Why this changes the research direction

The earlier F1c observational result selected EVERY in 4/4 truthfully sensed early-shift worlds and FOURTH in 4/4 blind-feedback worlds, but its periodic route actually had higher per-output CIL compute cost. In stationary ecology F1c's **inverted success** control selected EVERY and generated **614** full-budget correct service writes vs truthful FOURTH's **539**, even while receiving **worse** information. Now the cost direction is source-extracted and confirmed with actual paid step accounting. Thus F1c was **not** evidence that the actor was choosing a rational compute-saving information policy; it was a limited source-authored feedback-sensitive branch controller.

### Falsifiable break-even for a real charged authenticated sensor

Suppose a **new** generic, actually charged response-serial/provenance verification sequence (e.g. B53/B54-derived, with its own trust limits) adds exactly `q` **meaningful CIL steps per sampled feedback**, to BOTH policies, leaving the original 3/8/15 costs and shared continuation otherwise unchanged. For four successful services:

- EVERY total `4(3+q) = 12 + 4q`
- FOURTH total `3·8 + (15+q)=39+q`
- FOURTH strictly cheaper **iff `q>9`** (integer `q≥10`), tied at `q=9`, EVERY cheaper when `q<9`.

**This is an algebraic prospective threshold only, NOT yet a native-verified authenticated sensor or a demonstrated evolutionary tradeoff.** Every claimed check instruction must perform a real service, native CIL `Read/Branch/Sub` etc, be paid, and be subject to B53/B54's stale/corrupted/current-nonce forged feedback limitations. Merely padding a monitoring path with busywork to cross q=9 is not scientific progress. Likewise, when feedback legitimately signals failure, original failure marker cost and opportunity costs alter quantitative break-even and must be accounted separately.

## Decision

Reject both as general improvements. No native promotion. A universal confirmation delay is another fixed researcher policy and cannot guarantee persistent gains under indistinguishable prefixes. The next step should not be more fixed-threshold tuning on these twelve revealed cases. Establish a decision-theoretic frontier using an explicit finite family of schedules, distinguish sampling cost from trial cost and delayed benefit, and measure regret against feasible non-clairvoyant policies. Derive a paid policy whose monitoring rate may change based on past outcomes, freeze its source and untouched test distribution before native experiments. Explicitly compare against initial-only and fixed policies, retain all negative cases; no future duration oracle. A bounded policy can still fail under adversarial changes. B3/B4 NOT_MET.

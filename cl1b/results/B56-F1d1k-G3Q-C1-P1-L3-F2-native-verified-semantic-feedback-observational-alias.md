## Interpretation and important limitations

This is a **real original-native impossibility/identifiability witness for the specified sensor set**: any deterministic decision policy that sees only this original actor-visible history must make the same decision in the two environments, even though one is successful and the other fails. Native event-origin authenticity does **not** authenticate semantic correctness of an external feedback bit.

It does not prove no possible digital organism can discover truth: an additional independent task observation channel, cross-check or interventions exposing verifiable outcomes can break this equivalence. Nor is it a proof of a new learning algorithm: the F0 founder and finite three-opcode grammar remain researcher authored, and the hidden goals are *external to the CL0 World* by design. The worlds are identical precisely because the hidden goals are not included in the native state. This is **not full G3Q-C1 or cyberlife**.

The next necessary step is to **combine source-paid structural certification with actual task feedback within one original PID**, then test task feedback deception separately from the structural coverage certificate; never confuse the two.

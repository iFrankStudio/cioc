## Which B56 hypotheses this does or does not support

**N56-A under strict no-preexisting-monitor criterion: NOT ACCEPTED; native-paid NEW SERVICE PATH ASSEMBLY mechanically verified**, with the strong qualification that the founder contains an author-authored exact assembler and pre-authored branch, not a source-derived mutation strategy. **N56-B: not tested**; this pilot did not prospectively assemble/evaluate/retain two distinct monitor topologies or beat a strong fixed monitor and blind control under paired useful-output metrics. In fact, static EVERY's 1,384 exceeds assembled A's 1,374 correct outputs under the same16k actual paid steps.

**N56-C: not tested**; the actor never edited its source generator/search-control routine in response to a new environment. The same byte-identical authored assembler was executed once per actor without evidence of changing its mutation rule. No resource acquisition and no adaptation outside the original affine program grammar is observed.

**B3/B4 NOT MET; cyberlife NOT ESTABLISHED.** B56-F0 establishes that the existing digital physics can pay for assembling a new live executable monitor without giving the host source-edit privilege, but it remains **purpose-built assembly rather than endogenous discovery**, and was not a useful competitor to the strong static baseline.

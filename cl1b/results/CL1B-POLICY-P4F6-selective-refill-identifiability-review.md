## What the existing comparisons cannot identify

The F6 source-identical “all PatchNext effects suppressed” arm is an intervention on **every** paid `PatchNext` effect, including failure-path activation and success-monitor arm/disarm, not a surgical intervention on the clean-streak quota refill. The F5-on arm is a **different executable program**, so its lower total cost is a legitimate net comparison but not a causal estimate of refill alone. Consequently, the observed 144 actor-paid quota refills, 231 paid online policy edits and 16,265 extra instructions versus F5 **do not identify the marginal benefit of the refill mechanism**. In particular, a beneficial edit enabled by a refill could be outweighed by repeated streak-monitor overhead; conversely, disabling all edits may save monitor cost without isolating the quota gate.

## Proposed decisive matched intervention (not yet implemented)

Hold the **identical loaded 180-node F6 CIL source, initial credits, task vector, feedback schedule and per-opcode charging** fixed. Compare original F6 against a native interpreter-level, audited intervention which *only suppresses the state effect* of the refill-specific `Write38=0` after a four-clean-success threshold, while charging the attempted instruction exactly as in F6. Keep `Write38=0` at initial setup (if any), the ordinary `Write38=1` budget consumption, all `PatchNext` effects, `Read39/Write39`, and actual `PatchConst PC122` behavior untouched. An alternative if distinguishing this exact dynamic write is impossible is a one-bit independent budget-write suppression hook scoped to the **PC of the refill instruction**, with trace assertions that no other write is affected. Avoid a different-source “no refill” program: that changes the instruction mix and cannot isolate the mechanism.

The counterfactual may change later PCs, edits, monitor arm/disarm counts, and stage attempts; **do not** force trajectories to stay identical after the first differing refill. Check exact prefix equality until that point, then compare genuine total costs and true task completions. Record the number of suppressed refill writes, subsequent quota checks, actual extra paid PC122 edits, failed stages, true service attempts, occupied units, stranded compute, and termination. A suppression that never fires is a *no-exposure* control, not evidence of equivalence. Because `Write38=0` may also occur on other paths, the intervention must prove it only targets the intended dynamic PC and precondition.

**Estimand:** within the selected task-sequence distribution, paired difference `paid_CIL_cost(refill_suppressed) − paid_CIL_cost(F6_original)`, subject to both completing the same number of real goals; otherwise report completion and cost jointly without treating early termination as cheap success. This is the **total causal effect of permitting quota replenishment under the same pre-authored monitor program**, not the value of an autonomously invented strategy. Report paired per-world signs and family-level heterogeneity, not only aggregate totals.

## Evidence gates before any new experiment

1. Freeze an executable protocol, intervention semantics, original-source hash, true new task vectors, all controls, and decision criteria **in a commit before the first new original Rust run**. The already exposed P4F6 vectors are exploratory/reused, not fresh holdouts.
2. Include identical-source unmodified F6, selective refill suppression, an unrelated-write placebo suppression (same charged attempted instruction), and a no-refill-exposure negative control; include truthful and inverted-feedback tests. Use matched task vectors with at least one genuinely new, unseen regime family or independently frozen generator seed. Do not choose a favorable mixture after viewing outcomes.
3. Use the original CL0 `16 data / 180 source / 300 occupied` limits and actor-paid instructions. If adding a generic native intervention facility, verify it does not mint credits, reveal future goals, mutate loaded source, or silently alter `Write` accounting.
4. Independently replay *both* arms at every paid CIL instruction: PC, registers, all data cells, credits, source properties (including `PC133.next`), occupancy and feedback after paid service. The independent interpreter must explicitly reproduce the intervention semantics and reject an unexpected target PC.
5. Archive failures and run provenance. A full passing CI, source-pinned original-native traces and independent replay are required before empirical conclusions. The older [run 44862741694](https://github.com/iFrankStudio/cioc/actions/runs/44862741694) validates the baseline only.

**Decision significance:** If selective suppression is not worse than original F6 on new holdouts, the paid refill facility has no demonstrated positive marginal value under this source/setting, even if its physical ability is verified. If original F6 improves true completion or paid cost, that is evidence for this **researcher-authored** refill rule in those conditions, not spontaneous learning or open-ended evolution. This review is deliberately not a commitment to a numbered next study; future work must re-evaluate live repository facts before deciding whether this falsifier is still highest value.

## Reproducible aggregation

The 20 equal-weight regime families divide into **2 positive** (`f5_second_late_shift`: +582 instructions/9 cases; `f6_permanent_pair_wide`: +900/9), **17 negative** (sum −17,747/153 cases), and **1 exact tie** (`stable`: 0/9). Total **−16,265 instructions/180 cases**, agreeing with the archived native report. Positive-family mean saving is **82.333 instructions/case**, negative-family mean loss is **115.993 instructions/case**; tie-family mean is zero. These are descriptive conditional means on investigator-chosen regimes, **not** estimates of future regime frequencies.

For a *hypothetical* distribution that preserves the within-group equal-family mixture of these two positive and seventeen negative families, and excludes the tie family, let `p` be probability of a positive-family case. Expected F5-minus-F6 instruction saving is `82.333333*p - 115.993464*(1-p)`. Break-even requires **p > 0.58486** (58.49%). If the tied family has any nonzero probability but the positive/negative conditional mix is unchanged, it scales the expectation toward zero without changing the sign threshold. This threshold is **not a measured prevalence**, is **not a policy usable by the actor**, and cannot justify selecting favorable regimes after seeing results.

In the **four new preregistered P4F6 families only**, the wide-pair family saves +900/9 = +100 instructions/case; the three other new families jointly lose −4,710/27 = −174.444 instructions/case. Under a hypothetical mixture retaining equal weights *within* those three negative families, the wide-pair prevalence would have to exceed **174.444/(100+174.444) = 63.56%** to break even. The actual frozen new sample weights each family 25% and has net −3,810 instructions. This is a prospective design sensitivity calculation, **not a post hoc claim that the environment naturally favors wide pairs**.

## What this changes scientifically

A mechanism can recover edit quota and occasionally lower paid cost while still losing on the preregistered mixture. A claim of **general cost advantage** is unsupported without an independently justified target-environment distribution. Conversely the observed aggregate loss does not prove pointwise dominance: two regimes are favorable. A future trial should freeze the environment-generating process and regime weights *before* selecting the actor policy, score **all** original CIL instructions and true task completions, and include same-source edit-suppressed plus compact fixed-policy controls. It must not tune its mixture using the two already identified positive families and then call the resulting performance independent generalization.

No fresh experiment was run and no additional CI PASS is claimed by this documentation-only audit. The original 542-world, 752,269-instruction independent replay is still the evidence level; the actor's authored monitoring/refill rule is not self-invented, and B3/B4/open-ended evolution remain unproved.

## Falsifiable hypothesis

Both edits must be causally conditioned on fresh actor-observed evidence, not a host-supplied two-edit script or final safe genotype.

The I5 frozen finite semantic table predicts a shortest graph distance of two for each of the eight cases. That is a **reachability prediction**, not a source-size or execution-feasibility result. ## Required native arms and rejection criteria

- All eight hard genotypes: two actual paid source mutations and two complete witness resets, with no post-load host source/data intervention except protocol-permitted task feedback.
- **One-edit cap**: none of the eight may certify or serve hidden tasks; otherwise the frozen one-edit bound or certification is wrong.
- **Patch disabled**: all eight must fail to certify; patch attempts cannot count as mutations.
- **Fixed two-edit baseline**: compare success and charged instructions to an actor paying two preselected changes, so evidence-selected branching has to demonstrate a causal advantage on at least one blinded permutation, not merely eventual success.
- **Stale-witness ablation**: omit the reset between certifications and require that the observer detects any false approval; a false positive invalidates the safety claim.
- **Adversarial feedback**: invert Read20 after genuine task service; report objective truth separately from claimed success.
- Preserve strict original world limits: 16 data addresses, 180 CIL nodes, 300 occupied units, 8,000 initial private credits (or justify a prospectively frozen resource rule before implementation).

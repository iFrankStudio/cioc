# CL-1B B56 — paid source-generated monitoring topology, not another seeded policy menu

**Prospective research design, 2026-11-17. NOT IMPLEMENTED, no native B56 result.** This follows the measured [B55b native source-rate revision result](../results/B55b-native-paid-online-monitor-rate-revision-pilot-audit.md): one PID1 really executed 16 meaningful monitoring-rate changes in hidden regimes, but its researcher-authored controller spent many credits reapplying unchanged graph settings and **did not beat two strong fixed monitoring policies on total correct-output throughput**. Such a result is Tier1 authored-policy execution, not life or a self-created metapolicy. Parent [B55 Tier2 criteria](B55-endogenous-monitor-meta-policy-variation-preregistration.md) are preserved.

## B56 question and irreducible definition of novelty

Can one continuous, finite-resource native CIL individual **construct a useful monitoring-control topology that is not present in its initial executable graph as a complete, ready-to-route policy**, validate it by charged native trial/output evidence, select it with its own paid source edits, then **later revise the very construction/search/monitor-control routine** in response to changed costs or environmental feedback?

Merely choosing a different next-node among completed researcher-authored EVERY/FOURTH/NEVER subgraphs (B55a/B55b) **fails the B56 novelty criterion**, even if the choice is paid and correct. Likewise, pre-seeding a “policy constructor” that deterministically emits the same known winner in every environment remains evidence for *authored assembly*, not self-originating useful metapolicy. The only source of a limited novelty claim must be the **verified causal provenance** of a newly created, functionally distinct control graph that did not exist as an executable complete controller in the founder and was not injected by the host.

## Evidence tiers that must never be conflated

**N56-A — actor-paid assembly feasibility (lower bar):** allow generic CIL `CloneNode`, `ReadNext`, `PatchNext`, `PatchConst`, `Add`, `Sub`, `Branch`, and ordinary source/data I/O. Founder may seed **individual generic instruction templates and a task-blind mutation/assembly grammar**, but no complete useful monitor controller. An actor-origin run must charge for every constructed node, edge, feedback Read, code edit and failed candidate and produce a reachable novel executable monitor, which an independently written Rust graph analyzer must demonstrate was absent in founder. This alone is *authored synthesis*, not B3.

**N56-B — causally selected and retained novel policy (higher bar):** same PID1 must prospectively evaluate at least two source-generated, structurally different live monitoring designs in the **same** environment under equal charged opportunity, choose using only truly actor-observed evidence, patch its live route, and achieve strict paired improvement on preregistered useful-service-per-compute and error-cost criteria versus a source-identical **blind-feedback** assembler, a **do-nothing/source-edit-disabled** control and best strong preauthored static policy. Keep a privileged task-aware oracle separately marked; it may win.

**N56-C — second-order self-revision (candidate B3 component only):** after a hidden ecology shift, require the agent to edit **its own generator/selection/monitoring-control subgraph**, not merely its task solver or the selected service monitor, assemble and evaluate a second unexpected control topology, and show a causal later improvement under actual finite resource scarcity. Demonstrate an earlier policy genuinely abandoned, an updated mutation/search rule executed, a revised policy retained and not trivially equivalent to a preassembled hidden correct controller. A single hard-coded if/else “switch if failure” is not N56-C.

Neither N56-A, N56-B nor N56-C alone establishes open-ended evolution, self-maintenance or cyberlife. **B3/B4 NOT MET** until a complete independent acceptance threshold is separately evidenced.

## Frozen anti-leakage, topology and intervention controls

1. **Founder source inventory** must be published and hashed before any native task data. Identify every reachable/dormant complete functional policy. Treat unexecuted complete dormant source subgraphs as *seeded preexisting policies*, not actor-invented topologies.
2. **Generic instruction grammar** must be frozen before hidden-task and holdout observations. Do not add an `AutoAdapt`, `CorrectFeedback`, `RepairMonitor`, `Fitness` or any task-specific VM opcode. Document any researcher-authored reward or mutation generator, and separately test a blind/uninformative generator.
3. **Source authorship:** no host-origin `PatchNext`/`PatchConst`/graph replacement after process start, no PID replacement, no compute top-up, no hidden task label or target policy ID in actor-visible memory. Every first creation and later edit must be traced to paid `Origin::Internal(1)` native step and to a source node/cell the actor actually had.
4. **Neutral compute pressure:** explicitly price monitoring, source assembly, unsuccessful trials, storage and compute sensing. A negative outcome under strict resource limits is valid. An externally seeded false success/current-nonce forged response must remain a negative control rather than an oracle.
5. **True heldout alternatives:** require a stationary truthful environment, one previously unseen hidden task change and a later return, a withheld ecology with different feedback reliability or compute availability, plus different input-phase controls. Include at least one task family **outside the exact investigator-authored affine candidate generator**; if impossible under CIL primitives, record a capability boundary rather than supplying a task solution.
6. **Control equivalence and causal ablation:** evaluate identical source/memory/credit opportunity against a matched blind-feedback assembler, no-code-edit world, fixed monitor EVERY and FOURTH, no monitoring and privileged compact oracle. Time windows must include all failures, early stops and missing required outputs.
7. **Native/independent reconstruction:** record source SHA and prospective manifest SHA, charged PID1 steps, actual source instruction and edge alterations, full graph before/after for every mutation, data/cell provenance, instruction costs, heldout correctness, correct+wrong full-budget service throughput and paid-step recovery latency. Independently replay **every actual Rust-paid CIL instruction** with a separately authored source-parsing interpreter and fail closed at first divergence.

## Feasibility and capacity honesty

The B55b source already consumes **178 initial executable nodes**, then clones two more to reach the current180-node cap; it also contains complete authored monitoring policies. It **cannot simply serve as a valid N56-A founder** without replacing seeded complete controllers and freeing a genuine capacity for fresh source generation. Do **not** call re-routing one of those 178 nodes “novel creation”.

Before implementing B56, first commit a **separately numbered executable feasibility manifest** with exact neutral source grammar, frozen task family, deterministic seed/input schedules, intended CIL source/node/storage ceilings and per-world charged compute budget. Prefer the historical 4,000/16/180/300 ceilings only if newly generated structures genuinely fit; if they do not, **explicitly preregister a larger-world protocol before the first native execution**, disclose the resource difference and compare baselines under the same larger limit. Never silently increase budgets after inspecting outcomes, never discard failed seeds.

## Scientific stop criteria

If source novelty is just selection of a complete dormant precompiled policy, mark **N56-A rejected**. If a real novel source graph forms but cannot demonstrate independently verified functional benefit against fixed/blind controls, mark **N56-A mechanically feasible, N56-B rejected**. If policy selection works but mutation/monitor-generator source never changes, mark **N56-C not met**, regardless of the number of solved tasks. If native source and independent replay disagree, do not publish a positive finding.

# CL1B-POLICY-P4F0 — Prospectively frozen event-triggered policy revalidation

**PRE-NATIVE PROTOCOL ONLY.** This study starts from P3's exact-source original-native 56-world and 25,303-step verification, not from a claimed CL-1B completion.

## Hypothesis and falsifiers

An original PID1 can pay to edit **the control-flow edge of its own failure-count Write12**, so that further attention is charged only upon a failure rather than after every task. The newly armed event-driven detector pays for a two-failure threshold test and conditionally mutates executable source PC46 to a fallback stride1. This is a **researcher-authored conditional policy**, and costs of activation, repeated failures and every PatchConst remain charged.

Test full paired 24-task lifetimes under six frozen regimes, three starting goals, three first strides, both PatchNext-on and PatchNext-suppressed, plus inverted/forced0 feedback controls: **108 matched worlds + 2 controls = 110 native original single-PID worlds**. The second half of regimes, `holdout_revert` and `holdout_rare_shock`, is designated held-out relative to the exploratory pre-freeze choice of threshold2/fallback1; do not change sources or sequences after seeing outcomes.

## Causal boundary

The control **suppresses PatchNext alone**, not all source edits: P2 calibration still pays and modifies PC46 in both arms. Both pay the actor-source PC80–82 activation instructions and share identical initial source. The only causal edit initially distinguished is whether the failure-count Write PC12's next edge changes from 13 to 83. If enabled, after two paid actual failures in a subsequent task the original PID may pay PatchConst PC46. The host never edits source after loading and never gives hidden goals to the actor.

With unchanged CL0 physics, 91 initial source nodes, strict 16 data cells/180 source nodes/300 occupied, private credits6000, exactly 24 service stages, every original paid CIL opcode and delivered feedback must match a separately coded independent CIL replayer. A proof of correctly charged event-triggering **does not prove the fallback policy profitable**. Report net compute and task probe differences even if both are negative.

## Limits

No independently invented strategy, no privileged fitness oracle, no unified integration with I6U's structural repairs, no cross-world lineage, no autonomous reconstruction of a new graph. Inversion and forced zero feedback are adversarial controls, and truthful success must not be inferred from an actor's reward-only claims.

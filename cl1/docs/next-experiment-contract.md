# CL-1 Next Scientific Gate — Functional Dependency, Not Activity

**Status: future preregistration requirements, not an executed study.** Existing four studies returned a bounded negative/inconclusive result; this is a methodological refinement based on published outcomes, not a post-hoc reinterpretation of earlier experiments.

## Hypothesis that still needs a genuine test
Does an internally assembled multi-part digital organization maintain its own *functional relations* across disturbance and distinct observation epochs, without predefined repair roles or a host-level success oracle?

A valid experiment must define a *function of the whole* independently of the randomized seed, and then demonstrate dependency of that function on multiple **internally created** components. Merely having two processes share a cell or mutually change registers is insufficient.

## Necessary design improvements
- **Open non-teleological environment:** the externally supplied compute stream must be independent of current activity, with separate causal origin tracking. `run_scheduled` presently triggers at attempted CIL instructions, so a completely inert world cannot experience an independent later-world-time inflow. If an independent environment clock is introduced, its resource accounting and event provenance must be explicit; do not confuse the clock with organism aging.
- **More expressive information chemistry:** current graph, code and shared integer cell operations remain typed and fixed. Consider generic data/algorithm object interoperability and resource-rights transfer without adding `REPAIR` or `FITNESS` semantics.
- **Internal ancestry:** track origin of newly constructed executable code from concrete prior code/data objects, not just node-count changes. Separate externally authored initial diversity from internally generated novelty.
- **Functional ablation:** preregister interventions on an internally originated part and a budget-matched sham part; require downstream functional loss and **internal** recovery in two independent windows, beyond a coincidental restored edge.
- **Distributed closure:** preregister at least two distinct causal links whose reciprocal interventions reduce a joint independently defined functional indicator. Prove the coupling is not just scheduler time reallocation.
- **True holdout:** do not pick attractive seeds discovered in the first four studies and call their reruns independent confirmation. Freeze new seed ranges and functional metric before inspecting them.

## Positive acceptance must remain harder than negative feasibility
Before claiming CL-1 accepted, require: independently preregistered and source-pinned protocol, identical initial-state matched conditions, controls for direct exogenous restoration and resource injection, independent seed-world replication, multiple epoch evidence of internally initiated functional maintenance, identified bidirectional causal dependencies, code/data provenance, explicit effect sizes and uncertainty, full replay, a source-verified artifact and a report that includes every failed or inert world.

The experimenter cannot install a maintenance criterion into CIL or select offspring in the host to manufacture success. A biological likeness, body, wall time, or a fixed reproduction target is not necessary.

## Scientific stopping decision
If fresh studies remain null, close each *study* with an honest negative finding. Keep CL-1's **positive milestone open** rather than proclaiming cyberlife on the basis of complexity, longer execution or cloned code. A negative bound on one random program grammar is not a proof of impossibility.

## Study-06 finding and design correction (2026-11-13)

The first successful ablation of a genuinely internally constructed graph object produced **2/128** creator-process state differences but **0/128** cross-owner effects and **0/128** new nonempty candidate graphs after disruption. Only **16/128** seed-worlds had an eligible internal graph at all. This is not evidence of closure. [Registered Study 06](../studies/06-preregistered-internal-graph-ablation.md) and [immutable result report](../RESULTS.md#study-06--internally-constructed-graph-object-ablation-verified-2026-11-13) describe the experiment.

**Next required advance:** increase opportunities for *generic*, nonhost-directed code/data/object interactions; capture graph owner/reader history and object construction ancestry; then preregister a causal test of **function maintained by multiple internally generated components** with reciprocal targeted ablations, sham controls and two separated post-damage observation windows. No additional internal `REPAIR`, `FITNESS` or reproduction oracle should be created. A longer trace is not an organism.

## Study 07–08: exchange/provenance gap resolved, positive science still open

Study 07 added `GraphCopyFrom` and `GraphTransfer` with strict permissions/resource pricing. Across 128 previously unexamined seed-worlds, 13 graphs were eligible for ablation and **0** indirect cross-owner effects were observed. The preregistered *successful cross-owner exchange* outcome was **not evaluable** using the coarse event trace. Do not reinterpret it as zero after the fact.

Study 08 preregistered an event-level provenance model *before* implementation, then measured the original general grammar on fresh seeds. The interpreter successfully logs pre-action owners for completed copy and transfer operations, while trapped attempts cannot masquerade as success. However, **0/128** randomly initialized worlds exhibited successful cross-owner exchanges and **0/128** propagated the controlled ablation to other owners. This shows a currently sparse ecology, not impossibility of information exchange (the hand-authored mechanism fixture succeeded).

**Next gate:** object discovery and cross-owner information pathways must be possible through generic CIL information operations; record observer-only code/data ancestry, preregister a joint functional dependency independent of arbitrary process labels, and test reciprocal ablation and internally initiated maintenance across separated observation epochs, with independent sham and explicit externally restored controls. A positive effect or closed cycle of dependency alone must never be called life.

## Study 09: reachable opcode grammar is not sufficient exposure (2026-11-13)

Following preregistration, a larger 32-node generic 16-way instruction grammar added the missing graph read-grant and revocation operations, used fresh seeds and expanded compute opportunities. The 128 independent seeded worlds yielded **0 eligible nonempty internally created graphs** at the common intervention fork. As a result, successful cross-owner exchanges and graph-ablation consequences were **not meaningfully exposed** even though permissions were now expressible by the interpreter. This is a bounded negative **reachability** finding, not a negative result about life, mutual dependency or repair.

Do **not** simply increase random seed count for this exact trapping generator and call it a new organizational experiment. The [Study-10 neutral opportunity scaffold proposal](study-10-neutral-opportunity-design.md) specifies the next methodology boundary: generic code and data operations can be initially made executable as openly authored prerequisites, but such externally provided capacity must not count as spontaneously invented structure. A later preregistered study must isolate post-initialization functional organization and demonstrate reciprocal, internally initiated maintenance with real causal exposure, full null denominators and independently checked replay.

## Study-10 independent exposure calibration (2026-11-13)

The neutral graph opportunity scaffold solved the previous study's complete lack of exposure: **64/64** registered worlds had three nonempty graphs and readable published references; all **192 cross-owner copies** and **192 owner transfers** succeeded with provenance and charged resources. An external targeted deletion affected process state in 64/64 worlds, while the sham was equivalent and the zero-credit arm inert. **All these exchanges were specified by investigator-authored initial CIL and therefore represent calibration only.** There is still no observation of internally initiated functional repair or organizational closure.

A genuinely new study must first preregister a *post-scaffold* outcome that cannot be satisfied merely by executing the predefined prefix. Recommended contrast: matched worlds with identical prefix and resource budgets but distinct neutral continuation controls, and a functionally defined multi-component operation with two independent damage-and-recovery windows. Preserve internal/external code provenance, disable inherited scripted transfer/copy success as a scientific positive endpoint, and continue to report all negative or trapped trials. No life-specific repair or reproduction primitive is permitted.

### Study-10 post-scaffold continuation exposure (analysis amendment, 2026-11-13)

The registered random-continuation reachability measure was missing from the first version of the independent Study-10 report, not from the frozen protocol. The correction reran all ten study jobs. **63/64** untreated worlds successfully attempted at least one postscaffold randomized instruction; the analyzer counted 747 successful tail instructions and 148 trapped postfork attempts. These do not constitute internally discovered structure. This calibrated substrate makes it practical to preregister a stricter **Study-11** null test with a *function not already hardcoded by the prefix*, including explicit controls that disable the continuation while preserving the same initial prefix and resource endowment.

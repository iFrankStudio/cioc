# CL-1 Next Scientific Gate — Functional Dependency, Not Activity

**Status: future preregistration requirements, not an executed study.** Existing four studies returned a bounded negative/inconclusive result; this is a methodological refinement based on published outcomes, not a post-hoc reinterpretation of earlier experiments.

## Hypothesis that still needs a genuine test
Does an internally assembled multi-part digital organization maintain its own *functional relations* across disturbance and distinct observation epochs, without predefined repair roles or a host-level success oracle?

A valid experiment must define a *function of the whole* independently of the randomized seed, and then demonstrate dependency of that function on multiple **internally created** components. Merely having two processes share a cell or mutually change registers is insufficient.

## Necessary design improvements
- **Open non-teleological environment:** the externally supplied compute stream must be independent of current activity, with separate causal origin tracking. `run_scheduled` presently triggers at attempted CIL instructions, so a completely inert world cannot experience an independent later-world-time inflow. If an independent environment clock is introduced, its resource accounting and event provenance must be explicit; do not confuse the clock with organism aging.
- **More expressive information chemistry:** current graph, code and shared integer cell operations remain typed and fixed. Consider generic data/algorithm object interoperability and resource-rights transfer without adding `REPAIR` or `FITNESS` semantics.
- **Internal ancestry:** track origin of newly constructed executable code from concrete prior code/data objects, not just node-count changes. Separate externally authored initial diversity from internally generated novelty.
- **Functional ablation:** preregister interventions on an internally originated part and a budget-matched sham part; require downstream functional loss and **internal** recovery in two independent windows, beyond a coincidental restored edge.
- **Distributed closure:** preregister at least two distinct causal links whose reciprocal interventions reduce a joint independently defined functional indicator. Prove the coupling is not just scheduler time reallocation.
- **True holdout:** do not pick attractive seeds discovered in the first four studies and call their reruns independent confirmation. Freeze new seed ranges and functional metric before inspecting them.

## Positive acceptance must remain harder than negative feasibility
Before claiming CL-1 accepted, require: independently preregistered and source-pinned protocol, identical initial-state matched conditions, controls for direct exogenous restoration and resource injection, independent seed-world replication, multiple epoch evidence of internally initiated functional maintenance, identified bidirectional causal dependencies, code/data provenance, explicit effect sizes and uncertainty, full replay, a source-verified artifact and a report that includes every failed or inert world.

The experimenter cannot install a maintenance criterion into CIL or select offspring in the host to manufacture success. A biological likeness, body, wall time, or a fixed reproduction target is not necessary.

## Scientific stopping decision
If fresh studies remain null, close each *study* with an honest negative finding. Keep CL-1's **positive milestone open** rather than proclaiming cyberlife on the basis of complexity, longer execution or cloned code. A negative bound on one random program grammar is not a proof of impossibility.

## Mechanistic secondary and strict caveats

Only the `local` treated world showed **one** native-launched child with a verified successful non-`GraphCreated` operation. It is worth independent retrospective source/PC/opcode inspection, but it was **not** a successful shared-cell Write and must not be promoted to Study59's prospective primary endpoint. Local copies changed executable edge representation but failed to produce function-bearing child writes in all sampled worlds; therefore simply making edges locally in bounds is **not** sufficient for spontaneous functional child behavior under this stochastic grammar.

Every local-edge transport is performed **by the VM at the time of copying**, not repaired by a native organism.

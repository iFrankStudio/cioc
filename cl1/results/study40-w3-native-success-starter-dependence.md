## Critical paired diagnostic: does general ignition add no-starter activity?

| Frozen criterion | Study38 ambient alone | Study38 ambient+general | Study39 ambient alone | Study39 ambient+general |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Native success in ≥2 W3 epochs with **no same-epoch actor starter** | **22/128** | **22/128** | **21/128** | **21/128** |

Across the original matched seeds, the **exact same 22 Study38 worlds** and **exact same 21 Study39 worlds** meet the no-same-epoch-starter two-epoch criterion in both `ambient` and `ambient-general`; **zero general-only, zero ambient-only**. This was computed from the complete original Study40 per-seed archive and must be treated as a **post-result explanatory contrast**, not a separately preregistered prospective positive/null endpoint.

This sharpens the conclusion: general environmental ignition increases raw and successful native W3 activity, but in these two cohorts it does **not** add worlds that retain two late successful epochs without an actor-credit ignition action **in those same epochs**. The test still allows ambient resource input and any *earlier* host activation; consequently even the 22/21 count is **not an autonomy indicator**.

## Scientific boundary and next target

**CL-1 remains OPEN.** Study38/39 reproducibly established a generic **world energy-access physics** effect, and Study40 establishes that its extra late activity is strongly contingent on host actor activation. None demonstrates self-generated computation, endogenous code change, native functional repair after damage, heredity, or multi-component causal closure.

The next phase must stop treating mere W3 scheduling as a proxy for life. Research should prospectively expose **generic native code/data modification and internal resource acquisition** under bounded open environmental resources, define **pre-damage native function** without hand-authored goal routines, and require *internally initiated, source-audited restoration of that function* against sham, no-resource and explicit external-repair controls. Preserve native code ancestry, environmental input ledger and no-fitness host behavior. No `REPAIR`, `FITNESS` or `REPRODUCE` opcode or preprogrammed organism.

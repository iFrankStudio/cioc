**Scientific interpretation:** Existing native code editing demonstrably changes execution instructions in real descendants (already established Study104), but this prospective fixed-horizon causal experiment gives **zero** cases of native-mutating program yielding more children than the byte-identical mutation-disabled control; only three slightly fewer and 29 tied. This is a constrained negative/inconclusive outcome for **investigator-coded deterministic mutation toggling** and a fixed generic resource world. It is **not evidence against all natural selection**, not an ecological advantage estimate for arbitrary programs, not open-ended evolution or life. Its shared environmental mutator input and full self-copy chassis were designed by investigators, and real genotype fitness depends on scheduling/horizon, resource and storage limits. The paired source3/8 groups are not independent replicates.

**Next causal gate:** Demonstrate coexistence, lineage frequency change and functional survival effects in the *same world*, with both variants generated natively by actor-authored mutations, then independently frozen perturbation tests that distinguish instruction-cost selection from preprogrammed mutation alternation and deterministic scheduler order. The strict further leap is a **new prospective non-hand-programmed CIL genome generator** able to produce meaningful self-replicating organization or a fair mutation distribution; those are not yet established. **CL-1 remains OPEN.**

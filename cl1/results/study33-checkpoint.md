## Scientific interpretation

Study31 established register-level source2-tag→output-address causality in two old Study27 worlds using its particular random grammar, while Study32 demonstrated native resource uptake/finite execution extension in 86 different seed-worlds. **Study33 does not establish that the two are jointly used within a single functional lineage**: although many worlds perform successful native compute draws, none met even the registered source2-labelled output endpoint under the reused Study32 ordinary random grammar, much less a source-sensitive paired output after budget exhaustion. No natural two-component closure, self-regeneration after verified lesion, autonomous self-sustaining resource cycle or W3 internal persistence was observed. The *absence of strict output exposure* means the combined mechanism remains **NOT EVALUABLE as a functional-maintenance rate**, and it is scientifically incorrect to claim zero chance of emergence.

Research decision: preserve all results, **do not retune the seed list, random grammar or acceptance threshold**. A future scientifically necessary prospective test would require independently seeded opportunities for code-source-to-output and resource uptake to coexist and demonstrate a functional endpoint under competing resource constraints, then separately investigate endogenous multi-component maintenance without researcher-coded repair machinery.

**CL-1 stays OPEN.**

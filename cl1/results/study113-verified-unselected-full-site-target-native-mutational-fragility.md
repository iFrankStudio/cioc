## Interpretation: what materially changed

Study112 proved that an investigator-directed **known fast path** can produce inherited fitness-like offspring advantage under generic public +400 compute pulses. Study113 uses the *same broad human self-copy chassis and same generic energy pulses* but **does not preselect the beneficial mutation site or target**. The reproduction conclusion flips: **most full-code edge edits harm realized native child birth** even though true mutation frequently occurs, and 32/64 original seed programs actually produce children with and expressing different executable code. This is direct evidence of **high mutational fragility and a very small favorable local code neighborhood** in this exact CIL syntax/program organization. It cannot be solved simply by increasing random seed count or calling a native PatchNext event a beneficial novelty.

It does **not** prove universal inability of digital life to evolve: the original genotype/copy/mutator policy was researcher-designed, the site+target samples are parameters of that one architecture, and only two source environments and a fixed 16-epoch window were tested. An actor's own mutator targets can drift due to native code modifications; this is endogenous program behavior, but mutation mechanism itself was human-programmed. No unbiased universe-wide program generator, autonomous multi-part organization, repair/recovery under new damage, spontaneous code replication origin or open-ended genetic innovation was demonstrated. **CL-1 remains OPEN.**

**Next falsifiable mechanism:** independently preregister a larger neutral mutational neighborhood / partitioned genome and compare full-site lottery outcomes with a **matched-size and matched-compute-cost** negative control, without hand-selecting beneficial alleles, with native child source code reconstructed at birth and exact financial ledger. Do not infer that neutral padding alone solves natural evolution until a genuinely beneficial spontaneous mutation appears and persists through unselected lineage competition.

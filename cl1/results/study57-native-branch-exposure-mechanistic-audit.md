- Both episodes occurred without a same-epoch actor-specific external rescue in `ambient-only`, but original code/credits and the world's environmental energy supply remain externally endowed. **Neither child had a successful native Write.** These retrospective witnesses prove authentic exposure/order only, **not beneficial mutation or reproduction**.

**Study56 common topology-independent native assembly:**
- Seeds **3,900,116** (parent3, child4 launch tick39), **3,900,199** (parent3, child6 launch tick46), and **3,900,226** (parent2, child4 launch tick38) all completed identical native assembly gates in **both** branch variants. - Thus Study56's 3/3 paired common paths do **not** directly test the causal effect of a changed Branch target *before the native assembly event*. They cannot be counted as support for an uplift.

## Scientific conclusion and forward gate

Study55's two uniform-only assembly paths were **actually preceded by natively executed changed branch sites**. Study56's three common assembly paths were not. The failure to replicate an uplift is therefore compatible with **rare execution exposure and temporal composition**, not proof that the static topology could never matter. However the registered prospective difference remains a **failed replication**, and this retrospective exposure analysis **does not convert it into a positive causal result**.

Future prospective work must define **native exposure-aware causal contrasts before seed outcomes** (e.g., globally random program graph edges with registered execution opportunities and use of original source Write), rather than attributing rare child launch events to static code modifications that were never executed. Avoid optimizing random seeds, hand-programming assembly, or treating W3 computation as life. **CL-1 OPEN**.

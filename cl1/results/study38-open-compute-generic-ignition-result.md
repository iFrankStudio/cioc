Each ambient arm received exactly **8 externally supplied credits** at every epoch 1..23, independently of native activity. Each general or draw-gated starter moved exactly **one existing pool credit** to one selected zero-credit, unstopped actor, and no starter created net compute. Independent conservation required `inventory = 189 - externally removed initial credits + 8 × ambient input epochs - cumulative charged native attempts` in the non-refill arms. Explicit process-refill actions were audited separately, not counted as endogenous generation.

**Important scientific boundaries:**

- The general neutral access policy is an **external scheduler** that gives previously ineligible processes an execution opportunity; the subsequent CIL attempts are native, but activation itself is not organism-initiated.
- Even **closed + general** had 64/128 W3-active worlds with a strictly finite initial resource pool. This shows sporadic W3 activity can result from distributing remaining finite inventory across epochs, rather than energy self-generation or indefinite survival.
- **Ambient only** had 23/128 W3-active worlds: natural native `DrawCompute` paths can sometimes harvest externally introduced pool energy if actors retain credits long enough, even without an ignition policy.
- The higher W3 result of **ambient + general** does not demonstrate self-organization, persistence after a lesion, adaptive resource management, heredity or evolution. It is a reproducible property of the **environment** and initial random native programs.

## Scientific interpretation

Across Study49 and **fresh, preregistered Study51 replication**, non-goal-directed CIL programs occasionally *do* construct and modify a code graph before launching it (1/256 in discovery, 3/256 in independent replication). The kernel's generic compositional mechanisms are therefore functional and the sequence is not confined to a human-scripted fixture. Nonetheless, **no functional child shared-cell output, cross-process functional closure, native repair after damage or self-sustaining lineage has been shown**. Externally endowed initial codes and ambient/host credit interventions remain explicitly external.

The next research gate is to examine the copied source opcode classes, closed-edge ancestry and child executable behavior in these original archival cohorts before revising the random generator. Avoid goal-directed success constructions, old-seed outcome search, retroactive endpoint changes and treating a complete assembly path as reproduction.

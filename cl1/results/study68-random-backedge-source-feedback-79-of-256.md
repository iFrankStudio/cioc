## Native functional trace and exposure caveat

Original seed **4,500,002**, broad-low/high, writer PC3: at W2 epoch8/tick101 successful `CellRead(key7)` and native `CellWritten(key9,value3)` vs value8 from matched high; independently at W3 epoch16/tick197, a *new* native `CellRead` and successful native `CellWritten` at the same PC, again values3/8. Another source-pinned witness seed**4,500,003** writer PC5 shows the same W2/W3 causal output. Read-ablated low/high variants have **zero native Read** and identical Write PC/tick/value trajectories, excluding sham input as cause.

The extra native exposure check found **196/256** broad-low genomes executed and *took* at least one Branch whose nonzero edge differs from short's before or during W2; for **20** of the 79 primary-qualifying broad worlds, *both* low/high branch traces took such a changed edge **before the first qualifying W2 write**. This pre-W2-write exposure criterion was deliberately strict and does not establish that the other59 worlds never took a changed branch later. It must not be misreported as 79 demonstrated early exposures or as no causal topology influence; the real inference is the **predeclared paired genotype-topology contrast**, with mechanistic timing still to be separately audited if needed.

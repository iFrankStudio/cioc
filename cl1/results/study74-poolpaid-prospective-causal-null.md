## Resource and mechanism limitations

- Broad-ambient-on-low successful native `DrawCompute` appeared in **72/128** seed genomes (total native Draw events **1,015**) vs OFF **72/128** (**1,014** events). This +1 difference is the single native pool-paid entry.
- Broad-ambient-on-low has **124/128** final states with positive shared pool but no runnable actor, the same **124/128** in OFF. Thus the new policy is not sufficient to resolve *most actual deadlock trajectories* in this fixed random grammar, even though mechanism calibration under a pending native Draw was positive.
- This points to **the actor's current native PC/source state and timing** rather than absolute lack of available energy. Most zero-credit processes are *not parked at an eligible Draw*; a policy restricted to pending Draw cannot help them without violating the intentionally frozen no-host-directed intervention constraints.
- The program contains externally preinitialized generic sensor/output port registers and the researcher-endowed compute pool; ambient replenishment and the opt-in VM entry law are **EXTERNAL substrate features**. Even W3 source-tainted output does not prove self-repair, heritable function, multi-component interdependence or open-ended evolution.

## Scientific decision

Do **not** reclassify an observed `pool_paid_attempt` as organism energy creation, and do **not** change the primary endpoint or seeds after observing null policy effect. Retain the exact positive microscopic mechanism and the ecological no-effect as separate findings.

The next high-value question is what prevents finite sensor-feedback + native Draw from becoming an **internally maintained** architecture: actor's last runnable PC (mostly not Draw), random fatal error/branch loops, preservation of sensor/compute state across self-modified code and native generation, and whether multi-component source/information dependencies can be constructed under honest conserved compute. Any new local-resource scheduling law or native conditional waiting semantics must be preregistered in a **new seed cohort with exact negative controls**, never retroactively applied to Study74-P.

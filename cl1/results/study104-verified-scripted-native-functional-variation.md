## Strongest verified claim and limitations

**Strongest defensible claim:** Without new VM repair, reproduction, mutation or fitness primitives, a natively running CIL actor can change an active executable edge; the changed code is propagated by the actor's complete CIL code-copy sequence into a real child; that child runs a different number of paid instructions while retaining the real source-responsive read/write function; multiple descendants and siblings coexist and reproduce in a finite externally supplied compute world.

**Not demonstrated:** The 10:4 birth-allele distribution **is not an estimate of positive natural selection or of an adaptive mutant advantage**. The mutation target, toggle, founder replicator and finite compute budget are all human authored; the alternation and timing can determine the 10:4 ratio without any unselected genotype-environment interaction. Study101 separately proved a designed F6:S4 advantage under a finite shared pool, but simply juxtaposing two distinct scripted studies does not prove that spontaneously created beneficial alleles are naturally selected. No two mutually regenerating organizational components, autonomous functional repair, stochastic novel mutation distribution, or open-ended evolution is observed.

For stronger evolution claims, freeze a **new prospective ecological** native-genotype sampling or unbiased mutation process, remove the alternating human-coded fast/slow oracle, define genotype independent of fitness outcome before simulation, verify all original seeds/traps, and compare true heritable allele differential descendants under resource-limited mixed competition with fixed source/provenance, mutation-block controls and event-level genealogy. Archive original full-state SQLite snapshots and a separate read-only Python provenance auditor before making a scientific evolutionary inference. **CL-1 remains OPEN.**

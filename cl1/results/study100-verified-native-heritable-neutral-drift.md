## Scientific boundary and next gate

This is **human-engineered self-replication + authentic inherited neutral genetic drift**, not spontaneous genesis or adaptation. PC23 is a dormant otherwise unreachable Const: the drift is deliberately **fitness-neutral**. A single linear parent→child lineage lacks competition among coexisting variant siblings, and neither resource competition nor beneficial-vs-harmful variant differential proliferation has been established. Each genealogy is finite, externally energy-endowed and bounded by storage and compute. Six scripted worlds = **zero new independent randomly seeded ecological genotypes**; do not count the control worlds toward CL-1 positive sample size.

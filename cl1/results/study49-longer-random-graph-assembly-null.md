## Interpretation and forward gate

Study48 retrospectively found **no copy→edit→launch same-parent same-graph paths** in earlier random ecological cohorts. Study49 shows many opportunities for isolated native copying and launch but still no functional children. Before selecting another custom instruction distribution, pre-register a **read-only causal path diagnosis** on the original Study49 256 worlds, explicitly count copy→edit→launch lineage, and separate child executable structure from successful execution. A later prospective study must seek **generic, goal-neutral mechanisms of graph locality and native compositionality**, not smuggle in a hand-written viable organism or enlarge seeds until one lucky event appears.

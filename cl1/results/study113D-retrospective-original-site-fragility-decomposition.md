## Scientific consequence and next implementation gate

This retrospective breakdown identifies an experiment design constraint, rather than promising success by shielding selective site regions. The human-engineered active CIL copy+feedback core is fragile; it needs a broader viable mutational neighborhood for meaningful stochastic exploration. However adding deliberately dormant nodes and directing mutants to those harmless sites may raise mutant survival while simply accumulating neutral, unexpressed code.

## Scientific boundary and validity

The run data already existed before this analysis registration. Allele persistence itself is not evidence of *frequency increase due to selection*, since a HUMAN-coded graph-copy machine may deterministically replicate its code, and two original founder programs and native mutator sites/targets are all HUMAN specified. A specific allele appearing at least twice establishes transmission, not a genome fitness coefficient; a long linear copy chain is not an open-ended evolving population. The upcoming prospective gate would require **new unbiased mutation alleles in a mixed population with explicit frequency trajectory and multiple new mutation rounds** plus real functional maintenance and controls. Do not claim digital life, endogenous compute self-generation, autonomous novelty or sustained selection from this study. **CL-1 OPEN.**

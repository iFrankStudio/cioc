## Generic world physics under test

Introduce a **disabled-by-default** serialized world policy `recoverable_draw_shortage` without changing CIL opcode binary encoding, the legacy `DrawCompute` or legacy `pool_paid_draw` execution mode. In an enabled world:

1. If a **valid** native `DrawCompute` requests amount `r` from a common pool containing **less than r**, this is a *resource wait* instead of a fatal `Trapped`: the attempted instruction still consumes **exactly one** compute credit from its usual provenance (actor balance if positive, or world pool if pool-paid), PC remains on the original Draw, registers/source unchanged, actor is **not** stopped, and a native-origin provenance event `DrawDeferred {requested:r,available:pool_after_attempt_fee}` records the failed request. A bad operand, invalid next edge, overflow or non-Draw opcode STILL traps as before. The external environment does not choose a process or mutate its code.
2. To avoid an impossible zero-credit Draw loop consuming a public-pool fee while the amount is still unaffordable, **only when BOTH policies are enabled** the generic zero-credit scheduler may charge a zero-local-credit native Draw from pool **only if** the pool has at least `requested+1` credits; one unit pays the attempt fee, then `requested` is drawn by the native instruction. If the pool has less, no attempt occurs (no event, no credit charge) until the public pool changes. A valid `r=0` still requires one fee and may execute but cannot mint energy. Invalid/negative requested amount must not be made eligible at zero credits; it cannot bypass normal strict operand validation.
3. The law is a new explicitly researcher-given piece of **substrate opportunity**, not an internally invented retry routine. It cannot turn an actor with zero local credit and pending `Read`/`Const`/`Write` into a runnable actor, does not revive stopped actors, and does not create compute. Under any energy condition: `pool+sum(process.credit) = original pool+original process credit+all external pool input−native attempts`.

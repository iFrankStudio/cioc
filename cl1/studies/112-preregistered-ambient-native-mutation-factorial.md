## Prespecified primary 2×2 interaction outcome

For each original seed and source separately, compute:
`D_ON = native_births(mutation enabled, ambient ON) − native_births(PatchNext disabled, ambient ON)`;
`D_OFF = native_births(mutation enabled, ambient OFF) − native_births(PatchNext disabled, ambient OFF)`;
`I = D_ON − D_OFF` (difference-in-differences). Report **all 32** frozen `D_ON,D_OFF,I` triplets in order, positive/negative/ties and two-sided exact sign p over discordant `I`, separately per source. No favorable direction is a minimum acceptance requirement; a null is retained. This tests whether ambient generic supply **modifies the causal effect of the investigator-programmed executable mutation on within-horizon native offspring count**, not whether nature discovered a beneficial mutation.

Secondary independent checks: mutation-disabled actors reproduce normally where actual native Draw is affordable; source3/8 functional actor writer and native child source ancestry; enabled descendants can inherit and first-execute fast PC4.next5 while disabled descendants retain slow24; mutation events only PC8; genuine child/grandchild count, compute pool idle/DrawDeferred, per-epoch births; actual source-native event and each full SHA256 epoch. No endpoint retuning for missing exposures, no rescues of zero-resource processes, and no post-result alternative experiment horizons.

## Interpretation boundary

A strong positive `I` would be **direct in-cohort evidence of an interaction between general public resource supply and the reproductive payoff of a human-coded native functional mutation strategy**, without a VM fitness oracle. It would not imply self-created energy, spontaneous organism genesis, freely sampled mutation paths, endogenous repair/closure, natural selection in an unbiased mutation ecology or open-ended evolution. The replicator and its mutagen choice are HUMAN designed, the input energy remains EXTERNAL, and only a finite 16-epoch performance window is tested. **CL-1 remains OPEN irrespective of this engineered causal finding.**

# CL-1 Study73 — prospectively registered native compute-harvesting plus non-prewired information feedback

**Status: FROZEN PROSPECTIVE PROTOCOL; no Study73 native worlds executed at registration.** This study follows the Study62 pivot and independent Study63–71 verification of separate capabilities: random source-dependent code feedback is reachable with finite initial 300-credit endowment, but Study66's initial 16-credit native DrawCompute ecology showed 0/256 W3 and Study67 attributed its deadlock to 207/256 zero-credit runnable access plus 49/256 fatal pool-shortage traps. Study72 separately tests within-world sensor switching and is not used to select Study73 seeds or determine the Stage73 grammar. **Purpose:** test whether two independent substrate opportunities—random non-prewired backward control flow and native 8-credit batch compute acquisition—can coexist to sustain a *real native information-sensitive function past the initial local credit budget*, with no host process-specific intervention. This is an engineered generic compute quantum and external ambient environment, NOT biological energy generation.

## Predeclared random-genome population and CIL

- **128 independent new raw seed IDs 4,900,000..4,900,127**, each passed exactly once through the fixed Study71 SplitMix64 integer seed whitening before subsequent original 64-bit LCG draws. No seed exclusions and no pilot selection.
- One process per world; fixed 3-op generic CIL interface `Const(r1,8,next1)`, `Const(r2,7,next2)`, `Const(r3,9,next3)`, 24 **unbiased stochastic** CIL continuation opcodes at PC3..26 and `Halt` at PC27, **no guaranteed cyclic connector**, `with_strict_capacity(cells4,nodes32,total64)`.
- Exactly the following 7-choice random CIL grammar: 0 `Read(r0,address:r2)`, 1 `Write(address:r3,value:r0)`, 2 `Add(r0,r0,r1)`, 3 `Sub(r0,r0,r1)`, 4 `Const(r0,LCG%8)` (one further LCG draw), 5 `Branch(test:r0, nonzero:target,zero:next)` (one further LCG draw), 6 `DrawCompute(amount:r1,next)`. For Branch at PC3 nonzero target3; otherwise **broad** arm target `3+LCG % (pc-3)` and **short** arm target `pc-1`. Both arms consume **exactly the same** LCG draws and differ only in those native target operands.
- Initial actor credits **64**, initial external world compute pool **96** when ambient, **0** when closed; process register1=8 also fixes the Add/Sub increment (not silently changed across modes). The generic 8-credit successful DrawCompute pays one per-attempt credit and transfers eight existing pool credits, i.e. net local gain +7 on that instruction, with full conservation.
- Ambient world physics, when enabled, adds **8 externally sourced credits only to the world pool** at the **start** of each epoch1..23; zero external actor credit start/refill throughout. A `DrawCompute` with temporarily insufficient world pool can trap/stops actor, as before. Every charged attempt consumes one local credit regardless of success or trap.

## Frozen matched conditions and sample size

For each of 128 seed genomes: two native Branch topologies (**broad**, **short**) × input initial sensor value (**low3**, **high8**) × native draw intact versus `DrawCompute` replaced by `Const(dst:r1,value:8,next)` (**draw-disabled**) × external world-pool resources present versus absent (**ambient**, **closed**) = **16 matched arms**, **2,048 original native runs, 49,152 hashed epoch snapshots**, 24 epochs ×12 native attempts quota. Programs within matched arms differ ONLY in preregistered Branch backedges and Draw→Const ablation. Source cell7 and all original pool actions are exclusively `External`. No host restart, targeted rescue, lesion repair or fitness instruction.

## Two distinct frozen primary metrics

**Primary A: Native function beyond starting actor credits, requiring native compute acquisition.** Under paired `broad/ambient/draw-intact/low` and `broad/ambient/draw-intact/high`, the same genome must perform a fresh successful native Read(key7) in W2 (epochs8..15) and again W3 (16..23), followed by a native CellWritten(key9) at the same writer PC, actual values exactly +5 high minus low, no intervening overwrite of r0 by Const, independently audited actor/PC/epoch and source-to-register def-use. Before the **W3 output**, the actor must also have performed at least one successful internally originated `DrawCompute`, and its cumulative native instruction attempt count by that output must be **strictly greater than 64**, making output after exhausted initial actor-credit allotment impossible without native pool harvest or illicit external injection. The high and low matched worlds must both independently satisfy all conditions. All **128** original genomes are denominator, regardless of early halt, pool trap or no sensing. Report exact counts and Wilson95 interval; 0 valid.

**Primary B: Specific native energy-coupling causal control.** Among those satisfying primary A, compare each matched low/high broad world to its **draw-disabled** ambient counterpart. The draw-disabled branch must have **no native attempts after its first 64 charged instructions**, and no native W3 source-sensitive output; if either happens, reject provenance audit. Record all paired `broad` vs `short` Primary-A flags, exact discordance, native Draw successful/trap counts and cumulative external additions. This isolates *enabled native DrawCompute* under the same fixed pool rather than claiming the environment magically supplied actor credits. For clarity, successful energy uptake is not autonomous total energy creation or self-maintaining life.

## Boundaries and decision

A positive shows **native access to externally supplied energy can support a randomly sampled input-dependent finite function beyond initial local credits**, a stronger intermediate than Study71's 300 initial credits. It is still not maintenance of a self-assembled executable, self-repair, reproduction, ecological independence or life: initial port register values, initial CIL code, ambient replenishment and the native Draw quantum are engineered world rules. A negative identifies an inability to coordinate native Draw and feedback at this code length/initial credit under unchanged catastrophic pool-shortage semantics, not impossibility of ALife. **CL-1 remains OPEN.**

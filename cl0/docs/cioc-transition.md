# CIOC -> CL-0 evidence transition

Source baseline: `docs/research-status.md`, `docs/research-checkpoint-v0.16.md`, and individual `experiments/v0.1` through `v0.16` reports on main.

| Versions | Reusable lesson | Forbidden shortcut in CL-0 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0.1–0.3 | distinguish causal maintenance from mere graph cycles, account for budget, compare external and internal restoration | built-in repair routes, full templates, host-level functional acceptance oracle |
| 0.4–0.10 | damage to maintenance/decision programs matters; joint faults identify minimal cutsets | dedicated O/G/V/M slots, implicit search/verify/install scheduler |
| 0.11–0.13 | behavioral fault detection and majority votes can be fooled; correlated failure and canary dependence matter | host truth oracle, special-purpose audit/repair protocols |
| 0.14 | resource competition and variation can alter lineage survival in a restricted model | treating bounded adaptation as open-ended evolution |
| 0.15 | maintenance costs can outweigh gains under low damage, and reverse under high damage | host AUDIT/REBUILD instructions or selected optimal metabolic strategy |
| 0.16 | preinstalled redundant controllers can locally cover single failures but fail jointly | adding more fixed repair controllers to manufacture apparent closure |

**Supported:** historical synthetic demonstrations of limited causal dependence, bounded program reconstruction, limited diagnosis and adaptation under specified world rules.

**Not supported:** spontaneous organizational closure, spontaneous life, self-generated goals, open-ended evolutionary novelty.

Methodological commitment: preregister outcomes; use independent matched snapshots, negative controls, conservation checks, multi-seed replication when stochastic experiments are introduced, and provenance separating user interventions from internal actions. A constructed positive example is a VM correctness fixture only.

Stage-1 caveat: no self-organization hypothesis is tested. The fixtures demonstrate execution, budget blocking, scheduling and replay equivalence only.

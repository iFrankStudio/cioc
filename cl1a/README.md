# CL-1A — Population / generational evolution (ARCHIVED)

**Status: archived and paused as of 2026-11-15.** This is a scientific-route status, not a claim of stage acceptance. No new CL-1A studies should be launched by default.

This route comprises the pre-split CL-1 experiments and original evidence at [cl1/README.md](../cl1/README.md), [cl1/RESULTS.md](../cl1/RESULTS.md), [cl1/studies/](../cl1/studies/) and [cl1/results/](../cl1/results/), including Study01–Study140. Historical paths are retained **unchanged** to preserve references, protocol preregistrations, event/source hashes and verification links. Old filenames and references saying “CL-1” mean the historical pre-split CL-1, now designated CL-1A. Do not rewrite original results, rename old code/tests, or imply successful Darwinian selection.

Last major finding: Study140 exposed a phase/scheduling confound in mutation-specific reproductive-rate measurements, including at a second active code locus. Inherited, native executable mutation and generation of offspring were established mechanically; causal genotype fitness, self-organized life and open-ended evolution were not.

Reopening CL-1A requires a new explicit decision, preregistered work and independent audit of controls. **Active route:** [CL-1B](../cl1b/README.md).
